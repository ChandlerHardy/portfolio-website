Why folks trust us

One family, one standard

Odom Family Auto started the way most good shops do — in a driveway, with a father teaching his son which bolt to loosen first. Decades of wrenching later, the tools are better and the lift is nicer, but the rule hasn't moved: fix it the way you'd fix it for your own family.

And while the two of them are under a hood, Mom runs the whole show — the books, the phone, the scheduling, the paperwork that makes a title transfer painless. If you've already talked to the shop, odds are you've talked to her. She's the reason the receipts are organized and the coffee pot is never empty.

That's why every car we sell goes across our own lift before it goes up for sale. We're not flipping auction cars with a wash and a prayer. We pull the wheels, we check the timing components, we drive it, and we fix what we find. If something can't be fixed for the money, we don't list the car at all.

“We'd rather lose money on one car than lose a neighbor's trust for good.”

We're small on purpose. You'll talk to the person who actually turned the wrench, not a service writer reading off a screen. And when something isn't right after you've bought it, you call us and we make it right.

Paying for it

We keep this part simple. Most of our cars sit between $4,000 and $9,000 precisely so a working family can buy one without signing their life away.

  • Cash-friendly pricing. The number on the card is the number we take.
  • Financing conversations welcome. Bring us your credit union pre-approval and we'll work with it.
  • Refundable hold deposits. Need a few days to sort funds? A deposit holds the car.
  • Trade-ins considered. Even the rough ones — we'll tell you honestly what it's worth.
